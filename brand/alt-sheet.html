Warmer — alternative directions

The primary mark (five climbing dots) is on the first sheet. These seven symbols and one type-only option are drawn in the same palette and typeface.

A · Thermo-W
The W is a temperature trace: it starts cold and ends on the hot dot. Reads as a letter first, a graph second.
B · Sonar
Rings tighten around the target: you are getting warmer. Strongest as a pure symbol; works at tiny sizes.
C · Thermostat
A gauge with the needle swinging to hot. Literal and instantly understood, a little more utilitarian.
D · Horizon
A sun coming up through cold air: the daily ritual, warmed. Friendly and calm; good on stream banners.
E · Needle
A compass needle: red for warm, blue for cold. Says navigation and temperature in one shape.
F · Target pin
A map pin that is cold at the tip and hot at the head. Strong on mobile; echoes “destination”.
G · Signal
Four bars rising, like signal strength: the temperature meter as an icon. Bold and legible even at 16px.
H · Ramp wordmark
Wer
No symbol at all: each letter warms from cold blue to hot red. Distinctive in type-led spaces and easy to animate; needs the “W” alone as the app icon.
