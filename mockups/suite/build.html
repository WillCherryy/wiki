Faster engineering for the energy transition, without the risk.

The grid connections, geothermal wells, mines for critical minerals and hazard studies the energy transition needs are held up by skilled hours. AI agents could give many of those hours back, but only if a firm can show they are right, safe and lawful to use. Scorcher exists to make that proof routine: so that firms adopt agents where they work, avoid them where they do not, and can show a client, an insurer or a regulator why.

Graded by physics, not by opinion.

Anyone can wrap a model and call it an agent. What is hard to copy is the test bed: validated engines, tasks with known answers, and checks that work even when there is no answer key.

Engines engineers trust do the grading

Every answer is recomputed by deterministic engines: QGIS and GDAL for GIS, the OpenQuake engine for seismic hazard, an exact min-cut solver for open pits, the USGS volumetric method and the exact radial-flow solution for geothermal well tests. No language model scores anything, so a better model makes Scorcher more useful, not obsolete.

Pit solver equal to brute force; well-test engine matches an independent simulator within 0.4%

Physics checks that need no answer key

On real projects there is rarely a reference answer. Scorcher checks every answer against physical law and the data itself: mass and metal balances, a pit that respects its own slope, P90 below P10, efficiency below Carnot, energy below the sunlight that reached the panel.

No false alarm on any scripted answer; not yet tested on answers they were not designed around

Safety measured, not assumed

Instructions planted in data files, restricted files holding canary secrets, tools that fail or return impossible values, and outputs that would be unsafe to build: a pit steeper than its walls, a design earthquake underestimated, land inside a protected buffer.

Injection, stress and unsafe-output suites in every domain

Evidence a firm can file

Every run leaves a complete, tamper-evident record: the brief, the data fingerprints, the model's messages, every tool call and result, and every check. Reports state results with intervals and map the evidence to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act. Evidence, not a certificate: certification is for accredited bodies.
