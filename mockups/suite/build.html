Scorcher Build · applied-science agents

Agents for the energy transition

AI agents that do the groundwork on solar, wind and geothermal projects: they gather site data from authoritative sources, run proven engineering models, and hand you a report in which every number shows where it came from.

The model never makes up a number. It plans the work, calls the tools, checks the results and explains them. Every figure comes from a dataset or a validated engine, and every assumption is written down where you can change it.

01 / Solar

Every roof, every field

What would a PV system on this site produce, how certain is that, and does it pay back?

  • Data: PVGIS, NREL NSRDB, NASA POWER, ERA5
  • Engine: pvlib, the open Sandia photovoltaic library
  • Output: monthly yield with an uncertainty range, losses, payback range

02 / Wind

Read the wind

What is the resource at hub height here, which turbine class fits, and what do wakes cost?

  • Data: Global Wind Atlas, NREL WIND Toolkit, ERA5
  • Engine: windpowerlib; PyWake for wake losses
  • Output: wind rose, yield P50/P90, turbine comparison

03 / Geothermal

Heat from below

Is ground-source or deep geothermal plausible here, and roughly what could it deliver?

  • Data: Global Heat Flow Database, BGS, USGS, national surveys
  • Engine: GEOPHIRES (NREL); borehole heat-exchanger models
  • Output: temperature at depth, heat or power range, key risks

04 / Engineering

Where do agents actually help?

A research programme, not a product yet: we test LLM agents on real engineering workflows and publish what works and what does not.

  • Method: paired trials against engineers on the same tasks
  • Measured: accuracy, time saved, failure modes
  • Output: open results, then agents only where they earn their place

How we build

Useful because it is honest

  1. The numbers come from tools, not the modelDatasets and validated engines produce every figure; the agent orchestrates and explains.
  2. Every figure is labelledMeasured, modelled or assumed, with its source and the date it was retrieved, the same labels Scorcher Sky and Tree use.
  3. Assumptions are yours to changeLosses, prices and design choices are listed and editable; the report reruns.
  4. Validated before it shipsEach engine is tested against published reference cases; each agent against engineers on the same tasks.
  5. Pre-feasibility, not sign-offReports support decisions; they never replace a qualified engineer’s design or certification.
