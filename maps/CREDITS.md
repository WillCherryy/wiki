# Planet surface maps

Equirectangular maps (2048 × 1024, re-encoded as JPEG) that `skyobjects.js` wraps onto the planets of the Solar System and the Moon.

| File | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| earth.jpg | NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation, July (Reto Stöckli) | Public domain |
| earth-night.jpg | NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble (city lights) | Public domain |
| moon.jpg | NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (SVS 4720), LRO LROC WAC colour mosaic | Public domain |
| mercury.jpg, mars.jpg, jupiter.jpg, saturn.jpg, uranus.jpg, neptune.jpg | Solar System Scope (solarsystemscope.com/textures), based on NASA imagery and elevation data | CC BY 4.0 |

The Solar System Scope maps are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); they were resized and re-compressed, with no other change.
