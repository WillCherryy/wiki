Privacy policy

Last updated

The short version. wikilink has no accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics and no ads. Your progress lives on your device. If you use leaderboards or leagues, we store an anonymous player ID, the display name you choose and your scores so that other players can see them. You can delete that data at any time from the Me tab.

wikilink is run by (the "controller" for UK GDPR / EU GDPR purposes). Contact for any privacy question or request: .

3. Wikipedia and other third parties

  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia. Articles are fetched by your browser directly from Wikipedia's public API, so Wikimedia receives your IP address and the articles you request. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. wikilink is not affiliated with Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation.
  • Hosting. The site and its API are served by infrastructure providers (for example GitHub and Cloudflare) acting on our behalf. They may process data outside the UK/EEA under appropriate safeguards.
  • Fonts. We use your device's own fonts and load no third-party font services.
  • Payments and ads (not live yet). If we add optional paid cosmetics, payments will be handled by a payment provider such as Stripe, and we will not see your card details. If we add advertising, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before loading anything that needs it.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you clear your browser data or reset the game.
  • On our server: leaderboard entries and routes for up to 24 months, then deleted or anonymised. Your player record (ID, name, XP) until you delete it.
  • Server logs: per the hosting provider's retention, normally days to weeks.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR / EU GDPR you can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or export your data, and object to our processing. Because we do not know who you are, the easiest way is self-service: open Leaderboards → Me → Delete my online data, which removes your name, scores, routes and league memberships from our server. You can also email us with your Player ID (shown in the Me tab) and we will act on it. Your local data never leaves your device, so you can clear it yourself in your browser settings. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority.

6. Children

wikilink is a general-audience word-and-knowledge game and is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before using leaderboards or leagues, and never use a real name.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your Player ID acts like a password for your online data, so keep your backup code private.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where required, tell players in the app.
