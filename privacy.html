Privacy policy

Last updated

The short version. wikilink has no accounts, no tracking cookies and no ads. If switched on, we count visits with a cookie-free, aggregate tool that does not identify you. Your progress lives on your device. If you use leaderboards or leagues, we store an anonymous player ID, the display name you choose and your scores so that other players can see them. You can delete that data at any time from Settings.

3. Wikipedia and other third parties

  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia. Articles are fetched by your browser directly from Wikipedia's public API, so Wikimedia receives your IP address and the articles you request. See the Wikimedia Privacy Policy. wikilink is not affiliated with Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation.
  • Hosting. The site and its API are served by infrastructure providers (for example GitHub and Cloudflare) acting on our behalf. They may process data outside the UK/EEA under appropriate safeguards.
  • Fonts. Fonts are served from our own site, so no third-party font service ever sees your visit.
  • Payments. The optional Supporter pack is sold through a payment provider (such as Lemon Squeezy, Paddle or Stripe) that processes your payment, email address and tax details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. We receive only an order ID and the anonymous Player ID that was sent to checkout.
  • Analytics. If enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics receives your IP address to produce aggregate statistics and discards it. It does not build profiles or track you across sites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Ads (not live). We do not show ads today. If we add advertising, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before loading anything that needs it.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you clear your browser data or reset the game.
  • On our server: leaderboard entries and routes for up to 24 months, then deleted or anonymised. Your player record (ID, name, XP) until you delete it.
  • Purchase records (order ID and Player ID): for as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically around six years in the UK. They are kept even if you delete your online data, so a restored backup code is still recognised as a supporter.
  • Server logs: per the hosting provider's retention, normally days to weeks.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR / EU GDPR you can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or export your data, and object to our processing. Because we do not know who you are, the easiest way is self-service: open Settings → Delete my online data, which removes your name, scores, routes and league memberships from our server. Records of a purchase are the one exception, because we must keep financial records. You can also email us with your Player ID (shown in Settings) and we will act on it. Your local data never leaves your device, so you can clear it yourself in your browser settings. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority.

6. Children

wikilink is a general-audience word-and-knowledge game and is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before using leaderboards or leagues, and never use a real name.

7. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your Player ID acts like a password for your online data, so keep your backup code private.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, where required, tell players in the app.
