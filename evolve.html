    Your Tree

    Every daily animal you find joins your own branch of the tree of life, placed where the published synthesis puts it. Tap a name to open it in Scorcher Tree.

    Where the ages come from. Each ancestor’s age is the one Scorcher Tree shows: the central estimate of a published molecular-clock interval, or the minimum of a published fossil calibration. Where only a bound is known, the bound is used. The relationships are the Open Tree of Life synthesis. Ages are rounded to the nearest million years.
